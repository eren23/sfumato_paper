\section{Compute Spend}
\label{app:compute}

The experimental program ran on a single RTX~4090 spot/on-demand
substrate from Runpod (\$0.20/hr), with one OpenRouter judge sweep
inside the voting-rule-gap follow-up.  Total spend across all
phases is approximately \$20.5; Table~\ref{tab:compute} reproduces
the per-phase breakdown.  The four phases correspond directly to
the body of the paper: Phase~$1$ for the three-axis decomposition
(\S\ref{sec:axis1}--\S\ref{sec:axis3}) and consensus distillation
(\S\ref{sec:distill}); Phase~$2$ for the voting-rule gap and
verifier sweep (\S\ref{sec:voting_gap}); Phase~$2/3$ for the K2
schedule toggle (\S\ref{sec:commit_lora_k2}), the mode router
(\S\ref{sec:mode_router}), and the S0--S4 spike chain
(\appref{app:spike_chain}); and Phase~$4$ for the parallel
aggregation negatives surfaced in
\S\ref{subsec:parallel_negatives} and the cross-family substrate
audit underlying \S\ref{subsec:k2_scope}.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The full experimental record (training scripts, eval scripts,
LoRA adapters, derived datasets, raw per-problem JSONL outputs,
and W\&B run links) is publicly available.  All four LoRA
adapters are on the Hugging Face Hub under the \code{eren23/}
namespace:
\code{sfumato-llada-prefix-robust-v2},
\code{sfumato-llada-prefix-robust-v3},
\code{sfumato-llada-commit-v2}, and
\code{sfumato-llada-commit-v3}.
The two derived datasets are \code{eren23/sfumato-prefix-robust-gsm8k}
($59{,}784$ rows) and \code{eren23/sfumato-commit-mixture-gsm8k}
($109$ rows).
