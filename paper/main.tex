% ---------- Abstract ----------
\begin{abstract}
Hybrid pipelines that combine an autoregressive (AR) language model
with a discrete-diffusion language model (DDLM) for chain-of-thought
reasoning have produced mixed results in the literature, with
conflicting reports on whether AR planning helps DDLM denoising and
whether DDLM sampling diversity is useful.  This paper is about
the \emph{substrate properties} of current-generation DDLMs that
explain those conflicts --- not about whether the hybrid stack
beats a parameter-matched monolithic AR baseline on a math
benchmark, which on GSM8K~\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} at the AR scales
we use as planners it does not.  We argue the conflicts in the
literature collapse once the failure surface is decomposed: hybrid
AR/DDLM reasoning fails along \emph{at least three orthogonal axes} ---
interface-format brittleness, planner-content trust (which we
characterize as a \emph{phenomenon} over a two-rung capacity
sweep, not yet a fully-resolved axis), and sampling-diversity
preservation --- that can be characterized and intervened on
independently.  On GSM8K~\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} with
\LLaDA{}-8B-Instruct~\citep{nie2025llada} and
\Qwen{\{0.5B,1.5B\}}-Instruct~\citep{qwen2024report} we show: (i)
interface-format brittleness is fixable with a small
($r{=}8$)~\citep{hu2022lora} prefix-robustness LoRA, with a
measurable capacity tradeoff between no-prefix accuracy and
branch-vote ceiling; (ii) planner-content trust is
capacity-dependent in opposite directions across two planners ---
a $0.5$B planner improves at higher capacity ($+5$\,pp), while a
$1.5$B planner regresses by $13$\,pp --- with the caveat that two
capacity rungs establish the inversion is real but a third rung is
needed to fully resolve the curve; (iii)
format-augmented LoRA training \emph{expands} sampling diversity
($5/5$-branch agreement $51.5\% \rightarrow 47.5\%$), rather than
collapsing it; (iv) consensus distillation into the diffusion model
is design-sensitive, not architecture-limited: late-block answer-span
surgery fails to bridge the \ctwoc{}-vs-\cmaj{} gap (\ctwoc{}$=70.5\%$
across two iterations); earlier-block full-response surgery recovers
(\ctwoc{}$=79.0\%$, within sampling error of the $80\%$ pre-registered
target).  Disentangling ablations attribute most of the lift
($+4$\,pp) to where commit fires in the diffusion schedule, with
full-response training a smaller secondary effect ($+2$\,pp).
Branch aggregation (\cmajc{}) on top of the \ctwoc{}-fixed v3 design
reaches $82.5\%$, $+3.5$\,pp over base test \cmaj{}, indicating
param-time and compute-time consensus mechanisms compose rather than
substitute.
A \Qwen{0.5B} self-consistency baseline~\citep{wang2023selfconsistency}
($b{=}5$, $t{=}0.7$) reaches only $40.5\%$ on the same eval, vs
\LLaDA{}'s $79\text{--}82\%$, ruling out generic self-consistency as
the explanation for the diffusion \cmaj{} advantage.
Finally, \cmaj{} itself leaves $8\text{--}12$\,pp on the table
relative to an oracle ceiling, and this gap is structural: $17$
peer-class verifiers (text-bag, $0.5$B--$72$B chat-LMs, embedding
and reward models, process-feature MLPs, step-PRMs, symbolic
arithmetic) all underperform majority vote, while a
frontier-judge baseline (Claude Sonnet 4.5 with chain-of-thought)
closes $86\%$ of the gap (\cmaj{} $79.1\%$ $\to$ judge $85.3\%$,
oracle $86.3\%$ on $N{=}500$).  The mechanism is that \LLaDA{}'s
GSM8K failure mode is problem comprehension, not arithmetic.

A K2 sub-block ablation on the v3 commit adapter produces an
inverted-U around the v3 default
(\code{COMMIT\_N\_BLOCKS}: $k{=}3$ peak, $k{=}0$ adapter-off
$-1.7$\,pp, $k{=}4$ always-on $-3.2$\,pp), locating commit-LoRA as
a single discrete inference-time schedule toggle at the
load-bearing sub-block-$1$ boundary --- a strictly weaker mechanism
than continuous schedule-aware adapters
(TC-LoRA~\citep{tclora2025},
TimeStep Master~\citep{timestepmaster2025}).  Across the parallel
sub-problems of test-time aggregation, a consistent pathology
emerges: an offline-replay per-problem mode router with surface
text features ($-10$\,pp vs best fixed), a schedule-weighted
temporal-SC voting variant ($-4$\,pp vs \cmajc{}-vote, against the
positive-direction prior of Zhang et
al.~\citep{zhang2025timefeature}), a KL-anchored schedule-RLHF
mid-pilot on commit-LoRA (within CI on MATH-$500$ $N{=}50$ but with
a directional reversal of the unanchored mini-pilot), and a
LLaDA-$1.5$ base swap with frozen v$3$ LoRAs ($-2$\,pp) all land
as honest negatives or within-CI signals at our substrate scale.
We surface these alongside a parameter-matched plain
Qwen-$2.5$-$7$B AR baseline (which reaches $\sim 86.5\%$, beating
the hybrid \cmajc{} at fewer active parameters) as disclosure
rather than as targets.
All datasets and adapters are public on the Hugging Face Hub.
Total compute spend across all experiments is approximately
\$20.5 on a single RTX~4090 substrate (\appref{app:compute}).
\end{abstract}
