\section{Compute Spend}
\label{app:compute}

The three-axis and distillation work runs on a single RTX~4090
spot pod from Runpod at \$0.20/hr; the voting-rule-gap follow-up
(\S\ref{sec:voting_gap}) adds RTX~4090/A6000 GPU substrate and
OpenRouter judge calls.  The K2 schedule-toggle ablation
(\S\ref{sec:commit_lora_k2}), the offline-replay mode-router
spike (\S\ref{sec:mode_router}), and the S0--S4 spike chain
(\appref{app:spike_chain}) add a small Phase-2/3 line.
Total spend across all experiments is approximately \$17.
Table~\ref{tab:compute} reproduces the per-phase breakdown.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The full experimental record (training scripts, eval scripts,
LoRA adapters, derived datasets, raw per-problem JSONL outputs,
and W\&B run links) is publicly available.  All four LoRA
adapters are on the Hugging Face Hub under the \code{eren23/}
namespace:
\code{sfumato-llada-prefix-robust-v2},
\code{sfumato-llada-prefix-robust-v3},
\code{sfumato-llada-commit-v2}, and
\code{sfumato-llada-commit-v3}.
The two derived datasets are \code{eren23/sfumato-prefix-robust-gsm8k}
($59{,}784$ rows) and \code{eren23/sfumato-commit-mixture-gsm8k}
($109$ rows).
